02 WHAT IT DOES

A support team's worth of work, written down as it happens.

  • 02.1

    Answers first

    Triages on arrival: intent, language, priority, tags, sentiment. Researches your knowledge, past tickets and the customer profile, then drafts or sends a grounded reply with cited sources.

    triage research reply
  • 02.2

    Takes actions

    Looks up an order, refunds a charge, files a Jira bug. Anything with consequences waits for a human to approve it.

    lookup_order refund_charge jira.issue
  • 02.3

    Knows your docs

    Help articles plus synced sources, searched by meaning and keywords with local multilingual embeddings.

    Confluence Notion Jira Zendesk HC Files
  • 02.4

    One thread per customer

    Email via SMTP, forwarding or webhooks, an embeddable chat widget, Slack and Discord with two-way sync, and a REST API.

    email widget slack discord api
  • 02.5

    Built for teams

    Multi-tenant by design. Invite your team, set roles, and give every workspace its own channels, AI settings and branding.

    owner admin agent
  • 02.6

    Local first

    Postgres and Mailpit in docker-compose, no cloud account needed. Without an Anthropic key the agent runs on local heuristics.

    postgres pgvector mailpit
03 AUTOPILOT

Switch on autopilot after the tests pass, not before.

Policies decide what may auto-send and what waits for a person. Simulate any conversation against the real agent and run graded suites before you let it reply alone.

POLICY · billing v12 · edited by Mia
AUTO-SEND when │intent in [shipping.status, password.reset] │confidence ≥ 0.85 │sentiment > -0.4
ASK A HUMAN when │action = refund_charge and amount > €25 │customer.plan = enterprise
NEVER when │close a ticket tagged legal

05 SELF-HOST

Your tickets stay on your server.

MIT licensed. Postgres with pgvector, a local embedding model, and Caddy for HTTPS. Point a domain at a small VPS and you're done.

  1. 01 Any VPS with Docker recommended Hetzner CX22 and up. Postgres, trace and Caddy with Let's Encrypt.
  2. 02 Coolify Compose build pack. Domain, HTTPS and secrets are generated for you.
  3. 03 Render + Supabase Free test deploy. Sleeps when idle, fine for clicking around.
